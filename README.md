🩺 MediLife - Electronic Health Record (EHR) System
---

 Medi-Life Platform is a comprehensive digital record of a patient's medical history and health
 information. Unlike traditional paper records, Medi-Life is designed to be easily shared and accessed
 across different healthcare settings, allowing for better coordination of care. These systems have
 several advantages over their paper-based predecessors, including greater accuracy, efficiency,
 accessibility, and continuity of care. EHR systems improve patient outcomes by providing immediate
 access to comprehensive patient records, promoting improved care coordination, fewer medical
 errors, and more accurate diagnoses.

 ---
🚀 Features
 ---

 1. Centralized patient information platform
 2. Admin and user portals to serve to the distinct needs of healthcare providers and
 users, ensuring that each user group has access to the relevant tools and features
